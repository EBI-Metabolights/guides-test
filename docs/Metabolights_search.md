# MetaboLights Search

MetaboLights is a public repository for metabolomics data, and as such provides a way to search for studies and compounds of interest.

## 1. Search for studies

MetaboLights studies can be searched using a variety of criteria, including study title, study description, study accession, study type, and study status. 

To search for studies, use the search bar on the MetaboLights website. Enter your search query in the search bar and click the search button. The search results will be displayed in the search results page.

## 2. Search for compounds

MetaboLights compounds can be searched using a variety of criteria, including compound name, compound accession, and compound type. 

To search for compounds, use the search bar on the MetaboLights website. Enter your search query in the search bar and click the search button. The search results will be displayed in the search results page.

## 3. Search for spectra

MetaboLights spectra can be searched using a variety of criteria, including spectrum name, spectrum accession, and spectrum type. 

To search for spectra, use the search bar on the MetaboLights website. Enter your search query in the search bar and click the search button. The search results will be displayed in the search results page.

## 4. Advanced search

MetaboLights provides an advanced search feature that allows you to search for studies, compounds, and spectra using a variety of criteria. To use the advanced search, click on the "Advanced search" link on the MetaboLights website. The advanced search page will be displayed.

## 5. Download data

Once you have found the data that you are interested in, you can download it by clicking on the "Download" button next to the data. The data will be downloaded to your computer.
